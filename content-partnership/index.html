For Businesses

The Monthly Story.

A content partnership for businesses that need a steady stream of professional video and photography, without building a team to make it. One production cycle a month. We plan it, we capture it, we cut it, and we hand it to you ready to roll out.

How a Cycle Works
01
Plan
Half a day, mostly by email. We settle what we are capturing and why, then lock the date and who needs to be there. No long meetings, no homework.
02
The Day
One production day on location: setup, filming, and teardown. We capture b-roll and real people on camera telling the parts that matter, guided so they look good and sound good without performing.
03
The Edit
One day of post. Half a day to shape the first cut, and a half day for your revisions, so what you receive is finished, not nearly there.
04
You Roll It Out
Delivered ready to publish, or as organized raw footage your social team can clip and spread across every platform you use. One day becomes a month of content.
What a Month Could Look Like

No two businesses tell the same story, and no two months have to look alike. A few ways one production day becomes a month of content:

The founder's whyA proper sit-down with the owner in their space, lit and recorded so it carries. One interview becomes a hero film and a dozen short quote clips.
On the floorA morning inside the plant or workshop, the machines running and the hands at work, captured cinematically so the product feels made, not just sold.
A day with the teamThe people who do the work, recorded with real audio so their character comes through. Culture, hiring, and an about-us film from one day.
Out of the officeThe owner on a trail, at the lake, or on the course, thinking out loud away from the desk. Human and shareable, not corporate.
The give-backThe business showing up in the community, a sponsorship or a volunteer day, proof of values that reads as real because we were there for it.
Behind the serviceA firm or clinic explaining how they actually help, in plain language, turning an intimidating service into something that feels approachable.
The welcomeA warm, well-lit walk through the clinic, shop, or office, so a first-time visitor feels at ease before they ever arrive. Built for the website and ads.
The destinationFor tourism and hospitality, a cinematic look at the place and the season. One day on the property becomes a library of content that drives bookings.
How it worksA clear, properly recorded explainer of the product or process, answering the question customers always ask, ready for every platform.
The expert takeThe owner answering the real questions people search for, captured as a set of short, authoritative clips. A month of smart posts from one afternoon.
A moment that mattersA launch, a milestone, or a thank-you to customers, made with enough care that it stands out in a feed full of phone footage.
Pure atmosphereThe storefront at golden hour, the espresso pour, the tools on the bench. The textured b-roll that quietly makes everything else look better.
