How Much Does a Video for Your Business Cost?

Primary keyword: how much does a brand video cost

A professionally made video for your business in Ontario generally runs from around two thousand dollars for a single, simple piece into the low five figures for a story led film or a day of filming that produces several videos. The number moves with how much filming, story work, and finishing the project needs. Here is what actually drives it.

Most production sites will not answer this question. We will, because you deserve to walk into a conversation knowing roughly where you stand.

First, a quick note on names, because what you call the video changes what people quote you.

What these videos are even called

You will hear a lot of names for what is often the same job:

  • Brand video or brand story video: a short film about who you are and who you serve. This is the one most businesses actually need.
  • Explainer video: usually animated, often for software. Useful, but a different craft.
  • Promo video: a shorter, more direct piece meant to sell something specific.
  • About video: a brand video that lives on your About page.
  • Testimonial or customer story: your clients telling your story for you.

Knowing which one you want is the first step to an honest number.

The short answer, by type

A simple, single talking head or promo piece tends to start around two thousand dollars. A story led brand film with proper interviews, multiple locations, and a real edit usually lands in the mid four figures and up. A full day of filming built to produce several finished videos at once moves into the low five figures, and is often the better value per video. Exact numbers depend on your project, which is the whole point of the next section.

What actually drives the number

Days of filming and locations

One location and one afternoon is one number. Three locations across two days is another. Time and travel are the biggest single lever, and the service area matters. Filming in Peterborough is simpler than a day in downtown Toronto.

How many finished videos you need

A single film is one cost. The same day can often produce a main story video plus several short cutdowns for social. More finished pieces from one day is usually where the real value sits.

Story and scripting before the camera

The part that makes a video worth watching happens before filming. Finding the story, shaping the questions, planning the day. That work is what separates a video people watch from one they scroll past, and it is real time.

Editing, music, and finishing

The edit is where a film is actually made. Colour, sound, music licensing, motion and titles. Good finishing is the difference between footage and a film, and it is a meaningful share of any honest quote.

One polished film vs. a library of content

If you only ever make one video, make it the right one. But if you can plan ahead, a single well run day can hand you a flagship story film and a month of shorter pieces. Thinking in a library rather than a one off is how most businesses get the most from what they invest.

Why the lowest number is rarely the real cost

The lowest quote usually means less story work, a faster edit, and a result that looks like everyone else's. There is a place for quick and simple. Just know that what you are really paying for is whether people watch it, remember it, and act. A forgettable video is the most expensive kind, because it cost money and did nothing.

How to know what your project should include

Before you ask anyone for a number, get clear on three things: what the video is for, where it will live, and the one thing you want a viewer to feel or do. Bring those, and any honest studio can give you a real range instead of a guess.

FAQ

How much does a video for a small business cost in Ontario?

Generally from around two thousand dollars for a simple piece into the low five figures for a story led film or a full day of filming that produces several videos.

Why do video quotes vary so much?

Because filming time, number of locations, how many finished videos you need, and the depth of story and editing work all move the number.

Is it worth paying more for professional video?

The goal is a video people actually watch and remember. A lower number that produces something forgettable is the costliest option, because it spent money and did nothing.

What is included in a video production quote?

Typically story and planning, the day or days of filming, and the edit with colour, sound, and music. Ask any studio to break those out so you can compare honestly.

Can one day of filming make more than one video?

Often yes. A well planned day can produce a main story film plus several shorter pieces, which is usually the best value.
